# 03 · Competitor and Originality Research

JM Roofing 3rd Generation, unofficial Lumera prospect concept. Phase 0, 2026-09-30.

---

## 1. Method

- **Direct competitors:** 12 Southern California roofing contractors. Three came from the user's reference list (WesTec, Terrazas, Factor) and nine were found by searching LA and San Fernando Valley roofers. Two more supplied references are outside the area (Brown's, Peak) and were studied for gallery information architecture only.
- **Evidence:** each homepage or gallery was read with a page fetch and captured in headless Chrome at **1440×900** and **390×844** on 2026-09-30. The screenshots live in `docs/phase-0/evidence-local/`, which is git-ignored because it holds third-party content. They are for internal review only.
- **Caveat:** fetched summaries can misreport details. License numbers and claims below are what each site showed; they were not re-verified with CSLB. Before citing any of it outside Lumera, spot-check it.
- Nothing here is to be copied: no layouts, branding, wording, animation or assets. Each entry records the abstract principle only.

## 5. Originality risks in the locked experience, and the rules that answer them

| Risk | Why it's a risk | Rule for Phase 1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Apple-style product explosion on black** | The "exploded and isolated object" move is familiar from consumer tech, cars and footwear | The house **never disappears**: it dims in its LA daylight. Layers separate only along their real stacking direction. No floating in a void, no spin. |
| **Resemblance to OC's system diagram (A4)** | Same subject, same manufacturer | Order by job, show real edges and openings, and keep our own composition and camera. Compare against A4 in review. |
| **Test-lab look (A1)** | Implies performance testing | Keep the weather ambient, not rigged (no fans, rigs, gauges), and label it as an illustration. |
| **Storm spectacle** | Banned; fear-selling | No lightning, no destruction, no failing roofs, no competitor-bashing "bad roof" beat. |
| **Floating shingle particles, decorative weather** | Banned; teaches nothing | Particles are sparse and physical (dust, a few leaves, water). Every particle follows the wind or drainage direction the chapter is teaching. |
| **Blue-orange grading, thermal false color** | Banned cliché | Heat is shown with light quality and reflected rays, never false color. |
| **Generational trope** (C11) | Common in the category | Use only an owner-confirmed family story with specifics (00 row 27). |

**The "could it sell a jacket?" test, per chapter** (the brief's check that the idea is specific to roofing):

| Chapter | Roofing-only mechanic it depends on | Passes? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anatomy | Drip edge under the underlayment at the eave; flashing at openings; attic airflow | Yes |
| Heat | CRRC-rated granules; sun-activated sealant; soffit-to-exhaust ventilation | Yes |
| Wind | Sealed courses; nailing zone; starter edge; hip caps | Yes |
| Rain | Headlap; lap direction; drip edge into gutter; boot; kick-out | Yes |
| Reassembly | The last hip cap seats | Yes |
