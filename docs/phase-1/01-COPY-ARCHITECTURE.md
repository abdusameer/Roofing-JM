## 6. "BUILT FOR EVERYTHING." (locked, flagged)

- **Prototype:** shown exactly as locked, with a visible tag on the page: *Concept line · needs owner and legal approval before launch.*
- **Why it is flagged:** it is an absolute, objective-sounding performance claim placed right after an illustrated weather sequence and next to the CTA. Under the FTC substantiation standard (S32) it would need a reasonable basis, and no roof is built for "everything." The Owens Corning warranties are *limited* and name a wind figure (S12).
- **Safer production alternative (for review):** **BUILT FOR WHAT COMES NEXT.** It is softer and closer to puffery, but after the weather sequence it can still imply universal durability. Phase 0 rated a similar line "hold for counsel and owner review" (00 §4).
- **Strongest option for launch, if counsel prefers:** copy scoped to what the page demonstrates (sun, Santa Anas, the rainy season). Final wording is a Phase 2 copy task, not invented here.

## 7. Deliberately absent (Phase 0 prohibitions)

"Free" estimate · lifetime warranty (unqualified) · since 1964 / 62 years · insured · financing · solar · emergency / 24/7 · response times · prices · project counts · service-area city lists · hours · OC badges or "certified" · reviewer quotes. `tools/check.mjs` fails the build if the markup contains "lifetime warranty", "since 1964", "insured", "financing", "24/7" or "emergency".

## Photoreal rework note (2026-09-30)

- The roof is rendered in **Night Sky** (a dark Duration COOL color) to match the requested photoreal look. JM's own posts name **Oyster Shell**; the page says so in the opening note and the proof card. Night Sky's CRRC listing was **not** cited: two public sources gave different product IDs, so the only rating on the page stays the documented Oyster Shell one (CRRC 0890-0012).
- Drawn overlays (sun arrows, airflow lines, blue rain strokes, water paths) are gone. The weather is in the render; every explanation stays in the chapter text.
- The final section uses the reference's structure: "BUILT FOR EVERYTHING." over the golden-hour frame and an orange "GET AN ESTIMATE NOW" button (`--signal` #c2410c, white text 5.2:1).
